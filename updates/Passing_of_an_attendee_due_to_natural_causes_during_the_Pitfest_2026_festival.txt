TÍTULO: Passing of an attendee due to natural causes during the Pitfest 2026 festival

On Saturday, June 6, 2026, the tenth edition of the extreme music festival Pitfest, held in Emmen, Netherlands, recorded the tragic passing of an attendee. The incident occurred during the night, during the performance of the American band Gorilla Biscuits on the main stage.

💬 Conoce más detalles:
While the band was performing their concert, a spectator suffered a severe medical collapse and suddenly fainted at the venue. The response of the medical staff and the festival's first aid teams was immediate, initiating life support and cardiopulmonary resuscitation maneuvers on-site. Despite the rapid intervention and medical efforts, the individual's vital signs could not be restored, and their death was confirmed.

Subsequent evaluations conducted by medical authorities conclusively confirmed that the passing was due to natural causes, completely ruling out that the incident was related to trauma caused by the dynamics of the crowd dancing or substance consumption.

Emergency Management
Once the severity of the medical emergency was established, the organizers made the decision to suspend all live performances at the venue. This operational measure aimed to reduce noise interference, prevent crowd movement, clear evacuation routes, and allow paramedics to work without obstacles. Subsequently, the organization issued a transparent official statement to report the facts and stop any speculation on social media.

Final Statement from the Organization
To formally inform their community and express their condolences, the organization published the following official message:

"With great sadness we must inform you that one of our visitors passed away during Pitfest. During the Gorilla Biscuits concert, a person became unwell at the venue. Despite the quick intervention of the emergency services and medical professionals present, unfortunately help was in vain. It was later determined that it was a death by natural causes.

Out of respect for the deceased, their family, friends, and everyone affected by this sad news, we decided at that moment to suspend live music. Our thoughts are with the bereaved. Words fall short at times like this; we wish you a lot of strength and fortitude to process this loss.

We also want to express our gratitude to the rescue teams, staff, volunteers, and attendees who in any way helped during this serious situation. Pitfest has always been a place where people come together around music, friendship, and solidarity. Precisely because of this, this loss moves us deeply.

On behalf of the entire organization.

We extend our most sincere condolences to the family, friends, and loved ones of the deceased attendee in the face of this painful loss. Likewise, we recognize the work of the medical bodies, volunteers, and organizers for prioritizing the well-being and dignity of those present during the emergency.
